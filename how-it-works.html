HOW IT WORKS

Your hotel.
Your decisions.

You know your property and your guests better than anyone. You decide what belongs in the guide and what represents your hotel. We are there to research, advise, write, design and make it happen.

You stay in control throughout, without taking on another project to manage.

THE PROCESS

You set the direction.
We bring it to life.

01

UNDERSTAND

First, we listen.

We get to know your brand, your guests, your property and your neighbourhood. We listen to the people who understand the hotel best, so everything we create starts with your view of the guest experience.

02

SHAPE

You decide what belongs inside.

You tell us what you want guests to know about the hotel. Amenities, dining, offers, loyalty programs, experiences and practical information. We can advise on what may be useful, but the final content is your decision.

03

CURATE

You choose every recommendation.

We research the neighbourhood and bring you a proposed list of local businesses and experiences. It is a starting point, not a fixed list. Add businesses you already love, remove any you do not want, or change the list entirely. You have final say over every recommendation carrying your hotel's name.

04

CREATE

Once you set the direction, we do the work.

We turn your decisions into the finished guide. We write the copy, design every page, organise content and imagery, and handle outreach to the local businesses you have approved. We manage the moving parts while keeping you informed.

05

APPROVE

You approve the guide before print.

You receive the complete guide to review before production. Change the wording, imagery, information or recommendations if you want to. Nothing goes to print until your team is happy with it.

06

MANAGE

We manage what happens next.

Once approved, we coordinate production and delivery, manage the participating business relationships and organise restocks when you need them. You retain control of the guide, while the ongoing administration stays with us.

YOUR ROLE

Your hotel sets the standard.
Your team has the final say.
We make it happen.
