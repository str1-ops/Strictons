WHY STRICTONS

A better welcome.
More time for hospitality.

Your guests arrive wanting to discover everything your hotel and its surroundings have to offer. Your team wants to make their stay exceptional.

A beautifully designed Signature Hotel Guide brings those two things together. Everything guests need to feel informed, welcomed and inspired, thoughtfully placed in their hands from the moment they arrive.

THE OPPORTUNITY
01

Make every arrival feel considered.

First impressions are made in the smallest details. A beautifully printed guide, designed specifically for your hotel, adds a personal touch to the arrival experience. Something useful, thoughtfully presented and distinctly yours.

02

Less repetition. More hospitality.

Restaurant hours. Hotel facilities. Directions. Local recommendations. The same questions can occupy reception throughout the day. Give guests the information they need upfront, so your team can spend more time delivering the personal service that makes a stay memorable.

03

Make more of what your hotel already offers.

Your restaurants, bars, experiences and special offers deserve to be discovered. Give guests a clear introduction to everything available on-property, with carefully selected local recommendations that complement rather than compete with your hotel.

04

Your brand. Your standards.

Every guide is created around your property's identity, guests and location. You approve the content and every local recommendation. We handle the research, writing, design, production and ongoing management. A seamless extension of your hotel, without another project for your team to manage.

THE RESULT

A more thoughtful arrival.
Better informed guests.
More room for hospitality.
