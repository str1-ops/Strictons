HOW IT WORKS

Your hotel.
Our work.

You know your property and your guests. We take the time to understand both, then manage the process from there.

Your team stays in control of what represents the hotel, without taking on another project to run.

THE PROCESS

We do the heavy lifting.
You make the important decisions.

01

UNDERSTAND

We get to know the hotel.

We start with your brand, guest profile, property and neighbourhood. We speak with the people who know the guest experience best and understand what you want the stay to feel like.

02

SHAPE

We decide what belongs inside.

Together, we identify the hotel information that matters. Amenities, dining, offers, loyalty programs, experiences and the practical details guests regularly need.

03

CURATE

You choose who you recommend.

We research the neighbourhood and bring you a proposed list of local businesses and experiences. Add to it, remove from it or change it entirely. No business appears in your guide unless the hotel is happy to recommend them.

04

CREATE

Then we take it from there.

We design the guide, write the copy, organise the content and imagery, and manage outreach to approved local businesses. Strictons coordinates the moving parts so your team does not have to.

05

APPROVE

Nothing prints without your approval.

You receive the completed draft to review. Your team can request changes and approve the final guide before anything goes to print.

06

MANAGE

We stay involved after delivery.

We deliver the guides, manage the participating business relationships and organise restocks when your hotel needs them. The ongoing workload for your team stays deliberately small.

YOUR ROLE

Guide the decisions.
Approve the result.
Leave the rest with us.
