WHY STRICTONS

Give guests the answers.
Give your team the time.

Your guests want to know what is available, where to go and how to make the most of their stay. Your team wants to help them.

But answering the same questions all day is not the best use of great hospitality.

THE OPPORTUNITY
01

Make arrival feel considered.

Put the essentials, the hotel and the destination into every guest's hands from the moment they arrive. Clear, useful information means less uncertainty and a more confident start to the stay.

02

Give reception more room to be hospitable.

Directions. Dining hours. Spa details. What to do nearby. A well-designed guide can answer the repeat questions before they reach the desk, leaving your team more time for the conversations that actually need them.

03

Keep the hotel at the centre of the stay.

Your restaurants, bars, spa, offers and experiences should be easy to discover. Local recommendations sit around the hotel experience, not ahead of it, helping guests understand everything available to them.

04

Make it feel like your hotel.

Guests notice when something does not belong. Every guide is designed around your brand, your property and your guest, so it feels like another considered hotel touchpoint rather than generic visitor information.

THE RESULT

Better informed guests.
More present teams.
A stay that feels thought through.
