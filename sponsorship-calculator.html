HOW TO READ THIS

01

Potential audience is not an impression.

Each check-in can represent more than one person. The calculator first converts check-ins into estimated hotel guests, then separates those guests into people who actually look through the guide and the smaller percentage who then decide to visit your business.

02

Break-even matters more than reach.

If your average referred customer contributes meaningful gross profit, only a small number of incremental customers may be needed for the sponsorship to pay for itself.

03

Use conservative assumptions.

Until live tracking exists, keep guide-use and conversion assumptions modest. Real QR, click and offer-redemption data can replace assumptions over time.

Important: This calculator is an illustrative planning tool. Hotel check-ins and guest counts are not guaranteed impressions, and audience, conversion, customer spend and margin figures are user-entered assumptions. Strictons does not guarantee any number of visits, bookings, revenue or profit from a sponsorship.
